Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of multimodal representation learning and the data systems that make modern foundation models possible. I'm interested in how the structure of pretraining data shapes what a model learns, how we can build training pipelines that scale gracefully without large compute budgets, and how to evaluate cross-modal understanding in ways that go beyond benchmark accuracy. Increasingly I'm drawn to the messy, infrastructural questions — data curation, deduplication, pretraining recipes — because that's where the real leverage lives. A lot of what draws me to this side of ML is the same thing I like about my day job: the unglamorous infrastructure work where most of the real progress quietly happens.

Current Areas
  • Multimodal representation learning (vision, language, audio)
  • Data-centric ML and pretraining corpus design
  • Efficient training and inference at small-to-medium scale
  • Evaluation methodology for foundation models
  • Open-source research tooling
